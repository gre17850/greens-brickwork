Extension FAQs

Common questions about extension brickwork

Do you quote for extensions online?

Extension brickwork is priced after we have seen the job. Every extension is different, so we arrange a site visit to look at the work, discuss what you need and give you an accurate price. Send us the details to start the conversation.

Who supplies the bricks and materials?

Materials are purchased directly by the client, with us advising exactly what is needed and where best to source it. This keeps the process straightforward and gives clients full visibility over materials throughout the project.

Do you build single and double-storey extensions?

Yes. We carry out brickwork for both single-storey and double-storey extensions, from rear and side-return extensions to larger two-storey builds, working with your architect or main contractor where needed.

Can you match the existing brickwork on my extension?

Brick matching is one of our specialisms. We take care to select and lay brick that blends with your existing property, which matters most on period and older homes where a poor match stands out.

Which areas do you cover for extensions?

We cover Hampshire, Berkshire and west Surrey from our base near Odiham, including Basingstoke, Fleet, Winchester, Reading, Newbury, Camberley and Guildford, plus selected projects further afield.
