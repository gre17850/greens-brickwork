Brickwork contractors covering Reading & Berkshire

Reading is one of the busiest residential areas we cover — a mix of Victorian and Edwardian terraces, larger detached homes around Caversham and Tilehurst, and a steady stream of new build and extension work across Wokingham and Bracknell. We know how to work efficiently across all of it, whether that is matching original brick on an older property or building to spec on a modern development.

We work directly for homeowners and alongside contractors and developers. If you need a bricklayer in Reading for an extension, a garden wall, remedial brickwork or a full new build, we are worth a call. We give straight answers, turn up when we say we will, and finish the job to a standard we are proud of.

Wokingham and Bracknell sit well within our regular working area. We also cover Theale, Pangbourne, Tilehurst, Caversham and the surrounding villages — anywhere within reach of Reading is within reach of us.

  • House extensions — single and double storey
  • New build houses and residential developments
  • Garden walls, boundary walls and entrance pillars
  • Brick matching on Victorian and Edwardian properties
  • Remedial and structural brickwork
  • Contractor and developer work across Berkshire
