What we do

Brickwork, stonework, and bits in between

Whatever it is, if it's brickwork, we'll have a look. Small jobs we'll deal with you direct. Bigger ones we'll work for whoever's running the build. Same three of us either way.

New build houses

From the footings up to the chimney pots. We've done a fair few — working to the contractor's programme or straight for self-builders.

Extensions

Single-storey, double-storey, anything in between. We'll match the brick and the bond to the rest of the house so it looks like it's always been there.

Bricklaying & repairs

Repointing, patching, a bit of new wall. Small jobs are fine by us — we like them.

Stonework

Stone walling, feature work, and repairs. We're careful with older properties where the work needs to look right.

Garden & boundary walls

Usually done as part of a bigger job — alongside an extension or new build. If it's just a wall on its own, ask us and we'll see what we can do.

Working with main contractors

Happy to be brought in by main contractors and developers. We turn up when we say, work clean, and keep things to ourselves on private jobs. NDAs no problem.

Patios

Not our usual line of work — but if it's part of a bigger job, ask us and we'll have a look.

Questions

Things people usually ask

Where do you work?
Mostly Hampshire and Berkshire — Basingstoke, Reading, Winchester, Newbury, Windsor and the villages round about. We've gone further when the job's been a good fit, including private work in London. If you're not sure, just ask.
Do you do small jobs too?
Yes. A repair, a chimney repoint, one wall — all fine. Bigger jobs sometimes mean a bit of a wait, but we'll always tell you up front what we can do and when.
Do you run the whole build, or just the brickwork?
Just the brickwork. On smaller jobs we'll deal with you direct. On bigger ones we work for whoever's running the build — main contractor, developer or self-builder. We get on with our part properly so the rest of the trades can crack on.
Will you sign an NDA?
Yes. We've done a few private jobs in London where it mattered. If you need an NDA signed before we talk, that's no problem.
How long have you been doing this?
Over twenty years. New builds, extensions, stonework, repairs, walls — a bit of all of it.
Will you build me a garden wall on its own?
Usually we do garden walls as part of a bigger job. If it's just a wall on its own, ask anyway — sometimes we can fit one in.
What about patios?
Not what we do day to day, but if you ask we'll have a look — particularly if it's alongside other work.
Can you match the brick on an extension?
Yes. We'll find the right brick and mortar so the new bit blends with the old. That's part of the job.
How does pricing work?
On bigger jobs we quote our labour and you pay the merchant direct for bricks, sand, cement and the rest. No mark-up from us — you see what everything costs. We'll tell you what to order and where to get it. On smaller jobs we can quote everything in one figure if that's easier for you.
Are quotes free?
Yes. No charge, no pressure. Send us a few details or give us a call and we'll get back to you.
When could you start?
Depends what we've got on. Give us a call and we'll tell you straight when we could be there.
