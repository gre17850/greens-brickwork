What we do

Brickwork, flint work and stonework for quality building projects

We carry out brickwork for extensions, new builds, garden walls, structural work, flint work, stonework and selected repair or remedial work. We work directly for homeowners, developers, self-builders and contractors, including private projects where discretion is important.

New build houses

Full brickwork packages for new build houses, from footings through to completion. We work from drawings and site plans for developers, contractors and self-builders.

Extensions

Brickwork for single and double-storey extensions, including careful matching of brick, bond, mortar and detailing so the new work sits properly with the existing property.

Brickwork & remedial work

Selected repair and remedial brickwork, particularly where experience, matching existing work and doing the job properly matters. We may take on smaller brickwork jobs where they are the right fit.

Stonework & flint work

Traditional stonework, flint work, feature walls and repairs for period, character and country properties. We take care to match the existing style so the work looks right for the building.

Garden & boundary walls

Garden walls, boundary walls, retaining walls and decorative brickwork, built properly and finished to suit the property. Available as standalone work or as part of a wider project.

Working with main contractors

We carry out brickwork packages for contractors, developers and private clients. We have worked on confidential residential projects where professionalism, quality and discretion were expected as standard, and we are comfortable signing NDAs where required.

Patios

Patio work may be considered where it forms part of a wider brickwork, garden wall or landscaping project. Send the details and we'll let you know if it is something we can take on.

Common questions

A few things people usually ask

Where do you work?
We are based just outside Basingstoke, close to Odiham and Hook, and mainly work across Hampshire, Berkshire and west Surrey. This includes Basingstoke, Odiham, Hook, Fleet, Hartley Wintney, Winchester, Reading, Newbury, Wokingham, Windsor, Guildford and surrounding villages. We also consider selected projects further afield, including London, where the work is the right fit.
Do you take on smaller jobs?
We may take on smaller brickwork jobs where they are the right fit, particularly garden walls, remedial brickwork, matching existing work, flint or stonework, or jobs that require proper experience. For very small repairs, availability may be limited, but you are welcome to send the details and we'll give an honest answer.
Do you run the whole build, or just the brickwork?
Brickwork, flint work and stonework are what we deliver. On domestic projects we work directly with the homeowner. On larger builds, we deliver our part of the work for the developer, contractor or self-builder managing the wider scheme.
Will you sign an NDA?
Yes. We have worked on private residential and confidential projects where discretion was required, and we are comfortable signing an NDA before discussing details where needed.
How long have you been doing this?
Over 20 years. This is our first website, but not our first experience. We have worked across extensions, new builds, garden walls, structural brickwork, stonework, flint work, private homes and contractor-led projects.
Will you build a standalone garden wall?
Yes, where the job is the right fit. We take on garden walls, boundary walls, retaining walls and decorative brickwork, especially where the finish and durability matter.
Do you lay patios?
Patio work may be considered where it forms part of a wider brickwork, garden wall or landscaping project. Send us the details and we'll let you know.
Can you match the brick on an extension?
Yes. Careful matching of brick, bond, mortar and detailing so the new work sits properly with the existing property is part of the craft.
How does pricing work?
Materials are purchased directly by the client, with us advising exactly what is needed and where best to source it. This keeps the process straightforward and gives clients full visibility over materials throughout the project.
Are quotes free?
Yes. Send us the details and we'll give honest advice on whether the job is the right fit. Where needed, we'll arrange to view the work before giving a price.
