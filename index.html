What we do

Brickwork and stonework — from garden walls to whole houses

We're specialist brickwork contractors — not project managers. We work directly for homeowners on smaller jobs, and as subcontractors for main contractors and developers on the larger ones. Same team of three, same standard, whatever the scale.

Whole-house brickwork

Full brickwork packages on new builds — foundations through to chimney stacks. We work to main contractor schedules or directly for self-builders.

Extensions

Brickwork on single and double-storey extensions, with brick type, colour and bond matched seamlessly to your existing home.

General bricklaying & repairs

Repointing, repairs and new brickwork for any project — large or small. One-off repairs are genuinely welcome.

Stonework

Stone walling, feature stonework and stone repairs — including period and heritage-style work for sensitive properties.

Garden & boundary walls

Welcomed as part of a larger brickwork project — for example garden walls alongside an extension or new build. Standalone garden wall jobs considered on request.

Subcontract brickwork

Reliable brickwork packages for main contractors and developers. Discreet on private and high-end projects — NDAs welcome where required.

Patios

Patio laying considered on request — get in touch to discuss your project and we'll let you know if we can help.

Common questions

Answers before you call

What areas do you cover?
We're based in Basingstoke and cover roughly 50 miles in every direction, including the whole of Hampshire, Berkshire, Windsor and surrounding villages. We've also worked on private projects in London. If you're nearby, give us a call.
Do you take on smaller jobs?
Yes — small jobs are genuinely welcome. A one-off repair, a chimney repoint, or a single wall is just as worth doing as a full house. The only thing we ask is patience around larger projects we're already booked on.
Do you work as a main contractor or subcontractor?
We work as brickwork specialists. On smaller jobs that means dealing directly with the homeowner. On larger projects we usually work as subcontractors under a main contractor or developer who's managing the wider build. We don't project-manage the whole job.
Are you comfortable with NDAs and private projects?
Yes. We've worked on private London houses where confidentiality matters, and we're comfortable signing NDAs on request. Discretion is part of how we work.
How many years' experience do you have?
Over 20 years of brickwork experience — new builds, extensions, stonework, repairs, walls, the lot.
Do you build garden walls on their own?
Garden walls are most often part of a larger brickwork project we're already on — for example alongside an extension or new build. Standalone garden wall jobs are considered on request, so do still get in touch.
Do you lay patios?
Patio work is available on request. It isn't our day-to-day focus, but get in touch with the details and we'll let you know if we can help.
Can you match existing brickwork on an extension?
Yes. Matching brick type, colour and bond on extensions is part of the craft — we source bricks and mortar to blend with the existing property.
How do you handle pricing and materials?
For larger jobs — house builds, extensions and similar — we quote our labour, and you (the client) pay suppliers directly for materials. It keeps everything transparent: there's no mark-up from us on bricks, sand, cement or anything else, and you see every cost yourself. We're happy to advise on quantities, specifications and where to buy from. On smaller repairs and one-off jobs we can usually quote all-in if you'd prefer.
Do you offer free quotes?
Yes — all quotes are free and no obligation. Send us the details through the contact form or give us a call.
How quickly can you start?
Lead times vary by season and job size. Get in touch and we'll give you an honest answer on when we can be on site.
