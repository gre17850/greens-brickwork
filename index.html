What we do

Brickwork and stonework, for projects of every size

From a single repair to a full house build, every project gets the same care and attention. We work directly for homeowners on smaller jobs, and alongside main contractors and developers on larger ones.

New build houses

Full brickwork on new build houses, from the footings up to the chimney pots. We work to main contractor programmes, or directly for self-builders.

Extensions

Brickwork on single and double-storey extensions. We match the brick, bond and mortar to the existing house so the new work sits in seamlessly.

Bricklaying & repairs

Repointing, repairs and one-off brickwork. Smaller jobs are very welcome — a chimney repoint or a single wall is just as worth doing properly.

Stonework

Stone walling, feature work and stone repairs. We take particular care on older and period properties where the work needs to sit right.

Garden & boundary walls

Retaining walls, boundary walls and decorative brickwork — built to last. Welcome both as standalone projects and alongside larger builds.

Working with main contractors

Brought in by main contractors and developers as the brickwork team on larger schemes. Reliable on programme, tidy on site, and entirely comfortable with NDAs on private and high-end projects.

Patios

Patio laying considered as part of a wider brickwork project. Get in touch with the details and we'll let you know.

Common questions

A few things people usually ask

Where do you work?
Mostly across Hampshire, Berkshire and west Surrey — Basingstoke, Reading, Winchester, Newbury, Wokingham, Windsor, Guildford, Fleet, Andover and the surrounding villages, plus the New Forest area and affluent villages near Southampton. We also take on subcontract and confidential brickwork in London for main contractors. If your job's a bit outside that, get in touch and we'll let you know.
Do you take on smaller jobs?
Yes — smaller jobs are very welcome. A repair, a chimney repoint or a single wall is just as worth doing properly. If we're already booked on a larger project, dates may need a little flexibility, but we'll always be honest about that up front.
Do you run the whole build, or just the brickwork?
Brickwork is our trade and our focus — that's the part we deliver. On smaller jobs we'll deal with you directly. On larger ones we work for the main contractor, developer or self-builder running the build, delivering our package while they coordinate the wider project.
Will you sign an NDA?
Yes. We've worked on private projects in London and elsewhere where confidentiality was important, and we're entirely comfortable signing an NDA before we discuss the job.
How long have you been doing this?
Over twenty years. New builds, extensions, stonework, repairs, garden walls — there isn't much in brickwork we haven't done.
Will you build a standalone garden wall?
Yes — garden walls and boundary walls are welcome both as standalone projects and as part of larger builds. Send us a few details and we'll give you a price.
Do you lay patios?
Patio work is available, particularly when it sits alongside other brickwork on a project. Get in touch with the details and we'll let you know.
Can you match the brick on an extension?
Yes. Matching the brick, mortar and bond so the new work blends with the existing house is part of the craft — we'll source what's needed.
How does pricing work?
On larger jobs we quote our labour, and you pay the merchants directly for materials — bricks, sand, cement and so on. That way there's no mark-up from us, and you see every cost yourself. We'll advise on what to order and where to get it. For smaller jobs we can quote everything as a single all-in figure if that's easier.
Are quotes free?
Yes — quotes are free, with no obligation. Send us a few details or give us a call and we'll come back to you.
When could you start?
It depends on what we've got booked in. Give us a call and we'll give you an honest answer on when we could start.
